## 什么是抽象提示词？

抽象提示词是一类能够引导 AI 生成**超现实、荒诞、反直觉**视觉内容的提示词。它们打破常规叙事逻辑，融合错位的意象、荒诞的场景与奇异的运动，产生令人出戏又欲罢不能的抽象视频。

## 精选提示词

### 赛博城市溶解
```
A hyper-dense cyberpunk city slowly melts like wax under an invisible sun,
neon signs dripping into rivers of light, pedestrians unaware as the streets
fold upward into a Möbius strip, looping forever, camera drifting forward
through the impossible geometry, glitchy ambient soundtrack.
```
分类：`cyber` | 标签：`城市` `融化` `循环`

---

### 椅子的独白
```
An ordinary wooden chair in an empty white room begins to slowly rotate.
As it spins, it multiplies — two chairs, four chairs, hundreds of chairs,
all rotating at different speeds, some floating, some sinking through the floor.
Sudden cut to black. Silence. Then a single chair falls from the sky into an ocean.
```
分类：`daily` | 标签：`物件` `增殖` `荒诞`

---

### 鱼群书法
```
A massive school of fish in a dark ocean forms Chinese calligraphy characters,
each stroke written by thousands of fish moving in perfect unison,
the characters dissolving into ink clouds before the next stroke forms,
underwater light filtering through the ink like stained glass.
```
分类：`nature` | 标签：`鱼群` `书法` `水墨`

## 如何贡献

我们欢迎所有人贡献自己创作的抽象提示词！

详见 [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)

**简要流程：**
1. Fork 本仓库
2. 在对应分类目录下新建 `.md` 文件
3. 按照[模板格式](./prompts/TEMPLATE.md)填写
4. 提交 Pull Request

## 社区规范

- 提示词须为原创或注明来源
- 禁止包含违法、歧视、暴力内容
- 鼓励中英双语提示词
- 生成效果越抽象越好
